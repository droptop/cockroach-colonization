# Audio recording brief

What actually needs recording, taken from the call sites rather than from memory.
Generated by auditing every `Snd.sfx()` / `Snd.loop()` / `Snd.wings()` call against
`audio/` on 2026-08-16.

## Status

**All 36 sound effects are placeholders.** Every `audio/sfx_*.wav` is pure-python
synthesis from `tools/generate_audio.py` — sine, triangle and filtered noise, 22 kHz
mono. Nothing has been recorded.

**But each is now its own placeholder, and every name is hooked up.** Save a recording
as `audio/sfx_<name>.wav` over the placeholder, reimport, and it plays — no code change
and no registration step. That is the whole point of the split described below.

**Music is real and does not need re-recording.** The three MP3s in `audio/music/`
(*Burrow Lantern*, *Lanterns in the Drain*, *Mire King Shuffle*, artist tag
`exoticindie603`) are actual tracks. Six levels share three tracks, so a fourth would
be welcome eventually, but that is a commission, not a foley session.

## The thing that was wrong — fixed in code 2026-08-16

Two placeholder files were carrying the entire game.

| Name | Jobs it does | What that sounds like in play |
|---|---|---|
| `thud` | **16** | The rat landing, the cat's paw, the wasp's dive, a projectile hitting, a locked exit, a blocked shield, a wall cracking, Granny's swatter — all one sound at different volumes |
| `squeak` | **13** | Every boss's hurt AND death: rat, cat, mantis, wasp, Spider Queen |

A cat and a wasp made the same noise when hurt, and being blocked sounded like a rat
landing. **Both names have since been split into the 19 hooks listed below, and `thud`
and `squeak` deleted outright.** The recordings are still wanted — but the structural
problem is gone, and the game already tells the five bosses apart.

## Technical spec

- **Format**: mono WAV, 16-bit. 22050 Hz matches what is there; 44100 is fine and
  Godot resamples. Keep it mono — everything is positioned by code, not by the file.
- **Level**: normalise to about -3 dBFS peak. Call sites already trim per-use with a
  `volume_db` argument (range in use: -8 to +6), so record flat and let the game mix.
- **Pitch**: do **not** record variations. `Snd.sfx()` applies ±8% random pitch jitter
  by default on every play, so one take covers repetition.
- **Length**: keep one-shots under 0.4 s unless noted. `step`, `crumb` and `bite` fire
  constantly.
- **Naming**: drop files in `audio/` as `sfx_<name>.wav`, replacing the placeholder.
  No code changes needed — the names below are the filenames.
- **Loops** (`wings`, `granny_spray`) must be seamless. They are started and stopped
  by code, so they need to survive arbitrary looping with no click at the seam.
- After adding files: `godot --headless --path . --import`, then
  `godot --headless --path . --script tests/audio_hooks_test.gd`.

## Priority 1 — the split names ✅ HOOKED UP 2026-08-16

**The code side is done.** All 19 names below now exist as real hooks, each pointing at
its own distinct (still synthesised) placeholder. `thud` and `squeak` have been deleted
outright — nothing calls them, and their samples are gone from the build.

That means **a recording drops straight in**: save it as `audio/sfx_<name>.wav` over the
placeholder, reimport, and it plays. No code change, no registration step.

`tests/audio_hooks_test.gd` now scans the source and fails if any name the game calls is
missing from the registry — `play_sfx()` returns silently on an unknown key, so a typo
used to be an inaudible bug rather than an error. It also fails on registry entries
nothing plays, since the export ships every file under the project root.

### What each one should become

Recording direction for the 19. They all exist and all play today; these are the
sounds that should replace the synthesised stand-ins.

### From `thud`

| New name | Where | Brief |
|---|---|---|
| `impact_heavy` | Rat landing a leap, cat pounce, wasp dive connecting | Big soft body hitting a hard floor. Weighty, low, a bit wet. Not a drum. |
| `impact_light` | Projectile hitting, generic small collisions | Sharp, small, dry. A pebble on tile. |
| `block` | Shield absorbing a hit | Tin-can clang. Cheap metal, rattly, comic — the shield is a bottle cap. |
| `locked` | Player touches a boss-gated exit | Dull refusal. A heavy door not opening. Should read as "no", not as damage. |
| `crack` | Breakable wall taking a qualifying hit | Concrete fracturing. Two versions in one take is fine — the game plays it per hit. |
| `guard` | Mantis shrugging off a frontal hit | Chitin on chitin. Hard, dismissive, short. |

### From `squeak`

One hurt and one death per boss, plus a telegraph cry for the three that have one.
These are the game's characters — five voices.

| New name | Creature |
|---|---|
| `rat_cry` / `rat_hurt` / `rat_death` | King Rat — rearing telegraph, hurt, death |
| `cat_hurt` / `cat_death` | Cat — yowl, not a squeak. Big animal. |
| `mantis_cry` / `mantis_hurt` / `mantis_death` | Mantis — dry, clicking, insectile |
| `wasp_hurt` / `wasp_death` | Wasp — angry buzz spike, higher |
| `queen_drop` / `queen_hurt` / `queen_death` | Spider Queen — chittering, layered, the nastiest of the five |

## Priority 2 — the sounds Harry makes constantly

Heard hundreds of times per session. Fatigue matters more than impact here: anything
with a sharp transient or a musical pitch becomes unbearable by the third minute.

| Name | Brief |
|---|---|
| `step` | Tiny chitinous tick on hard surface. Very short, very quiet, almost felt rather than heard. The most-repeated sound in the game. |
| `jump` | Small effortful scuff. Not a cartoon boing. |
| `bite` | Harry's default attack. Small wet snap, mandibles. |
| `whoosh` | Weapon swing. Air, light, no pitch. Nine weapons share it — a neutral swish serves better than anything characterful. |
| `hurt` | Harry taking damage. Short pained chirp. Must cut through everything else. |
| `death` | Harry dying. Longer (up to ~1 s), a small deflating collapse. Currently a descending tone. |
| `wings` | **LOOP.** Sustained flight buzz. Must loop seamlessly and sit under everything without masking `hurt`. This one had a bug that machine-gunned it for months, so it is worth checking in play once real. |

## Priority 3 — pickups and world

| Name | Brief |
|---|---|
| `crumb` | Small food pickup. Dry, papery, quick. Fires very often. |
| `fruit` | Big food pickup. Wetter, rounder, more reward than `crumb`. |
| `splat` | Enemy dying, breakable smashing. Wet burst. |
| `complete` | Level cleared. The only celebratory sound in the game; ~1 s is fine. |
| `sizzle` | Acid and toxic drips. Corrosive hiss. |
| `water_splash` | Water hazard. Used once, in the cat fight. |

## Priority 4 — Granny

She is a level-scoped hazard, not a boss, and she attacks wherever Harry is.

| Name | Brief |
|---|---|
| `granny_swat` | Fly-swatter cutting air then slapping a surface. Fast, plastic, stinging. |
| `granny_stomp` | Slipper on lino. Heavy, flat, soft-edged. Must be clearly distinguishable from `granny_swat` — these two are the pair most often confused in play. |
| `granny_eek` | Her startled shout. The only human voice in the game. |
| `granny_spray` | **LOOP.** Aerosol hiss, seamless. Starts and stops with the visible gas cloud. |

## Priority 5 — not yet hooked up

Recording these has no effect until the code exists; noted so a session can capture
them while the mics are up.

- **Per-enemy movement sounds** — a standing backlog item. Spider skitter, ant march,
  fly hover, rat scrabble. The brief's reasoning is that a player should identify what
  is approaching without looking at it.
- **Weapon-specific swings** — nine weapons share one `whoosh`. A knife, a rubber band
  and a thrown pebble should not sound alike.
- **Baby cockroach chirp** — babies follow Harry and currently make no sound at all.
